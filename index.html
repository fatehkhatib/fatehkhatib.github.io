Privacy Policy — Rakah Prayer Counter

Last updated: September 2026

Rakah Prayer Counter is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses and why.

Camera

Rakah Prayer Counter uses your device's front camera to count your sajdahs (prostrations) during prayer. This is how the app automatically counts your rak'ahs and sajdahs.

  • In a lit room, the app measures changes in brightness as you move into and out of sajdah.
  • In a room too dark for that — Fajr or Isha with the lights off, for example — on iPhones with Face ID the app instead uses the front depth camera (the TrueDepth camera that Face ID uses) to measure how far away the nearest surface is. Coming down into sajdah brings you close to the phone, which is how the app counts in the dark.
  • Depth data is used only as a distance measurement. The app does not create or keep an image or map of your face, does not use Face ID, and does not identify or recognize anyone.
  • Camera frames and depth readings are analyzed instantly, on your device, for this purpose only. They are never stored or transmitted.
  • Optionally, you can turn on "Record video as a backup check" in Settings. It is off by default. When it's on, the app records a temporary video of your prayer so you can watch it back and confirm the count yourself. This video is stored only on your device, is automatically deleted the moment you close the review screen, and is never saved to your Camera Roll or transmitted anywhere. In a room too dark to count by light, no video is kept.

Location

Rakah Prayer Counter uses your device's location, with your permission, to calculate accurate local prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) so it can correctly label and organize your prayer history. Prayer times are calculated entirely on your device using standard astronomical formulas — your location is never sent to any server or third party. It's cached locally on your device so it doesn't need to be requested repeatedly.

If you use the Qibla direction, the app also reads your device's compass, on your device, to point toward the Kaaba. The app's Home Screen widget uses the same prayer-time settings to show your next prayer; they are shared only between the app and its widget on your device.

Prayer History

Your prayer records are stored locally on your device only: prayer name, date, duration, and rak'ah/sajdah counts, together with how each prayer was counted — by light or by depth, the room's brightness level, the count before any correction you made, and when each sajdah occurred. The app uses these details only on your device, to count more accurately over time (for example, to decide how dark a room must be before it counts by depth).

This history is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere. Rakah Prayer Counter does not require or use any account, sign-in, or personal identifiers.

No Data Collection or Sharing

Rakah Prayer Counter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Rakah Prayer Counter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
