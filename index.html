Privacy Policy — Prayer Counter

Last updated: August 2026

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party. All prayer counts and history are stored locally on your device only.

Camera: The app uses your device's front camera to detect prayer movements by measuring on-screen brightness changes. Video is processed in real time, entirely on your device, and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.

Location: The app may request access to your location to calculate local prayer times. This is used and cached on your device only, is never transmitted anywhere, and isn't required for the app's core counting feature — if access is denied, counting still works, just without prayer-time labeling.

No account, contact information, or other personal identifiers are required or collected by this app.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer.
