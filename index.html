Privacy Policy — Prayer Tracker

Last updated: August 2026

Prayer Tracker is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses and why.

Camera

Prayer Tracker uses your device's front camera to detect your body position during prayer, by measuring changes in brightness as you move into and out of sajdah (prostration). This is how the app automatically counts your rak'ahs and sajdahs.

  • Camera frames are analyzed instantly, on your device, for this purpose.
  • By default, the app also records a temporary video of your prayer so you can watch it back afterward — for example to check your sajdah form. This video is stored only on your device, is automatically deleted the moment you close the review screen, and is never saved to your Camera Roll or transmitted anywhere. You can turn this off anytime in Settings ("Record video for review").

Location

Prayer Tracker uses your device's location, with your permission, to calculate accurate local prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) so it can correctly label and organize your prayer history. Prayer times are calculated entirely on your device using standard astronomical formulas — your location is never sent to any server or third party. It's cached locally on your device so it doesn't need to be requested repeatedly.

Prayer History

Your prayer records (prayer name, date, duration, and rak'ah/sajdah counts) are stored locally on your device only. This history is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere. Prayer Tracker does not require or use any account, sign-in, or personal identifiers.

No Data Collection or Sharing

Prayer Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Prayer Tracker does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
