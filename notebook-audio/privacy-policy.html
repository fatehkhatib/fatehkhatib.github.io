Privacy Policy

Notebook Audio · Effective October 3, 2026

Notebook Audio is designed to keep your audio and notes private. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

The audio files you add, their titles, categories, playback positions, finish dates, notes and takeaways are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to any server we operate — we don't operate any.

iCloud Drive

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app can back up your audio and a list of its titles, categories and takeaways to your own iCloud Drive, in a folder named “Notebook Audio”. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backups off in the app’s Settings and delete the folder at any time in the Files app.

Files you import or export

When you share a file to the app, import from Files, or export to a location such as Google Drive, the transfer happens on your device through iOS. Any service you choose to export to handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Links shared to the app

If you share a direct link to an audio file, the app downloads that file from the link’s address so it can be saved. Nothing else is sent with that request.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not use third-party SDKs.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
